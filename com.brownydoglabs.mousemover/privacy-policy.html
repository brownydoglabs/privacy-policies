1. Overview & Core Philosophy

Mouse Jiggler (Package: com.brownydoglabs.mousemover), developed and published by Browny Dog Labs, is built upon an uncompromising Local-First and Privacy-by-Design architecture.

The application simulates mouse movement by placing a physical optical, laser, or infrared computer mouse directly onto your mobile device's screen. The app uses high-contrast geometric animations and optional gentle haptic vibrations to keep the mouse sensor active and prevent your computer from entering standby or sleep mode.

No direct connection to your computer: Mouse Jiggler is completely air-gapped from your PC, laptop, or employer's network. It does not install drivers, does not connect via USB, Bluetooth, or Wi-Fi to your computer, and has zero visibility into your computer's files, keystrokes, active programs, or network traffic.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • No Personal Identifiers: We do not ask for or collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, contacts, or user identities.
  • No Computer or Device Telemetry: We do not track what computer you use, your employer, your presence status, your working hours, or your browsing activities.
  • No Location Data: The application does not request or access GPS or coarse location permissions (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION or ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION).
  • No File, Camera, or Audio Access: The app does not request access to your files, photos, camera, or microphone.

3. Local-Only Execution & Device Safety

All animations, movement cycles, rhythm profiles, and vibration sequences are processed locally on your Android device:

  • Pure Local Rendering: Dynamic visual patterns (Checkerboard, Zebra Stripes, Turbo Matrix, Red Grid) are drawn directly on-screen in real time using native Android Canvas APIs.
  • Stealth Jitter Mode: The randomized interval algorithm (±3s jitter) runs entirely in local memory to prevent predictable activation cadences.
  • Hardware & OLED Protection: Target zone shifting and temporary pulse brightness prevent screen burn-in and save power. When in standby or paused, rendering loops are completely halted with zero CPU overhead.
Absolute Device Sovereignty: At no point during active jiggler operation is an internet connection required to run animations, vibrations, or timers. Core jiggler features can be operated entirely in Airplane Mode.

4. Device Permissions Used

The app requests only the minimum set of permissions strictly necessary for its physical operation and license management:

Permission Purpose
android.permission.VIBRATE Enables calibrated haptic micro-pulses in Vibration and Hybrid modes to gently nudge physical mouse sensors that do not track on smooth glass.
android.permission.WAKE_LOCK Keeps the screen awake during an active jiggling session, preventing the mobile device itself from sleeping while operating.
android.permission.INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Used exclusively for in-app billing verification (RevenueCat / Google Play Store) and serving ads (Google AdMob) in the free tier.

5. Third-Party Services & Advertising

To support ongoing development and offer a functional free tier, the application integrates standard, reputable third-party SDKs:

A. Google AdMob & User Messaging Platform (UMP)

In the free version of the application, banner and occasional interstitial ads are delivered via Google AdMob.

  • GDPR & EU User Consent: For users located in the European Economic Area (EEA) and the UK, the app uses Google's certified User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK to collect and manage privacy consent choices in compliance with GDPR and ePrivacy directives.
  • Ad Identifiers: Google AdMob may process device advertising identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID), IP addresses, and diagnostic performance metrics in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.
  • 100% Ad-Free in PRO: When upgrading to Mouse Jiggler PRO, Google AdMob is completely deactivated: no banners, no interstitials, and no ad network requests are made.

B. RevenueCat & Google Play In-App Billing

We use RevenueCat to manage in-app subscriptions and lifetime PRO licenses through the Google Play Store.

  • RevenueCat processes anonymous app user identifiers and receipt validation tokens to verify your PRO entitlement.
  • No credit card, banking, or financial billing information is ever seen or collected by the developer; all payment transactions are securely handled directly by Google Play.
  • For more details, review RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

6. Free Tier vs. PRO License

  • Free Tier: Includes essential movement modes (Display & Vibration), preset timers (15m, 30m), and standard speeds. Displays non-intrusive ads (banner and occasional session interstitials).
  • PRO License (Lifetime or Monthly Subscription): Unlocks unlimited session duration (∞), Stealth Mode (±3s random interval), 4x Ultra speed, Hybrid operation, exclusive patterns, and permanently removes all advertisements and ad-related network traffic.

7. Data Retention & Deletion

  • App Preferences: Your selected mode, rhythm profile, pattern, and PRO license status are saved locally on your device via Android DataStore / SharedPreferences.
  • No Cloud Accounts: Because there are no user accounts, passwords, or centralized cloud databases, there is no remote profile to delete.
  • Complete Data Erasure: Uninstalling the application immediately and permanently removes all stored preferences and cache files from your device.
